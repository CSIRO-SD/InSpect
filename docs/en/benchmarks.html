InSpect Benchmarks

Open taxonomic recognition

This setting evaluates zero-shot, fine-tuned and unseen-taxon recognition using cropped insect images, label-derived metadata or their combination.

Reported observation: unseen taxa remain challenging, and metadata provides useful but unstable contextual cues.

Fine-grained anatomical part segmentation

This setting evaluates supervised and text-guided segmentation of insect structures, including antennae, legs, wings and body regions.

Reported observation: thin structures remain difficult, and open-vocabulary models may confuse insect parts with the body or background.
